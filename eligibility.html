Who Can Donate?

Rules differ by country and donation center, but donors are generally expected to:

  • Be within the allowed age range
  • Meet a minimum weight requirement
  • Feel healthy on the day of donation
  • Wait the required time since their last donation

Who Should Not Donate?

  • People who have a fever, cold or infection
  • People who recently had surgery, a tattoo or a piercing
  • Anyone advised by a doctor not to donate

The staff at the donation center will do a quick health check and make the final decision.
