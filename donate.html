How Donation Works

  1. Register: Give your basic details at a local donation center.
  2. Health check: Staff check your health history and basic health readings.
  3. Donate: The donation itself usually takes only a short time.
  4. Rest: Relax for a while and have a snack and a drink before leaving.

Before You Donate

  • Drink plenty of water
  • Eat a healthy meal
  • Get a good night's sleep
  • Carry a valid ID if the center asks for one
