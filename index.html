Welcome

Donated blood helps hospitals care for people who need transfusions. Learning about donation is a helpful first step toward supporting your community.

Donation availability and eligibility rules differ by location. A local blood donation center can explain the requirements and answer your questions.

Learn how to donate

Why learn about blood donation?

Blood may be needed during medical treatment, surgery, or after an accident. Blood centers rely on eligible volunteer donors to help maintain supplies.
